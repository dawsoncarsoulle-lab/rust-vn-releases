Version 0.2.5 is in preparation, not yet published. Its planned terms for new original proprietary editor material are in [EDITOR-LICENSE.txt](EDITOR-LICENSE.txt). The Makepad framework, engine and other third-party software are **not** relicensed as proprietary.

Earlier versions included MIT notices. Nothing in the new license revokes permissions already granted for those copies or covered code. This is not a claim of exclusive rights over the whole historical codebase.

## Your games

You retain ownership of your original stories, scripts, artwork and other content. You may distribute and sell games made with rust-VN without royalties payable to rust-VN, including closed-source games. The editor license does not apply to your game merely because you used the editor.

Keep the applicable engine and dependency license notices with distributed runtimes. Obtain permission for every third-party asset you use. Included example assets are not all under one license: follow `EXAMPLE-CREDITS.md`, including attribution requirements.

The grants above do not override third-party licenses or rights granted by applicable law. They do not promise clearance of assets imported by users.
